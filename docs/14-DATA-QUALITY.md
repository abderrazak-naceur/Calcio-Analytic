# 14 — Data Quality

## Dimensions
Completeness, Accuracy, Freshness, Consistency, Uniqueness, Validity, Coverage.

## Checks
Missing matches, duplicate matches, stale provider, invalid odds, invalid timestamps, unknown teams/markets, score inconsistency e unexpected changes.

## Quality score
Score pesato per provider, competition e ingestion run con dettaglio delle cause.

## Reconciliation
Confrontare totali provider e canonical totals e rilanciare job incompleti.
