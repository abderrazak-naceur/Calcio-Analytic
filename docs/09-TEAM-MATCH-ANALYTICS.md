# 09 — Team & Match Analytics

## Team features
W/D/L, points per match, GF/GA, clean sheets, home/away split, rolling last 5/10 e season metrics.

## Advanced
xG/xGA, shots, corners, cards e strength of schedule quando disponibili.

## Match context
Kickoff, competition, standings al momento della partita, recent form, H2H e statistiche.

## Match page
Header → markets → odds timeline → team comparison → standings/form → statistics → historical context → model results.

## Historical correctness
Forma e classifica devono usare solo dati disponibili prima del timestamp della partita.
