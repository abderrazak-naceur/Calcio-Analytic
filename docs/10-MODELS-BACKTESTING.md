# 10 — Models & Backtesting

## Models
Phase 1 descriptive analytics.
Phase 2 ELO e Poisson.
Phase 3 Dixon-Coles, Bayesian e machine learning.

## Model registry
Ogni modello salva version, training window, feature version, dataset version e parametri.

## Backtest rules
No look-ahead bias, walk-forward evaluation, point-in-time features, historical market prices e dataset versionato.

## Metrics
Brier score, log loss, calibration, hit rate, ROI/yield quando applicabile, drawdown e sample size.

## Governance
La performance storica non deve essere presentata come garanzia di performance futura.
