# 08 — Odds Analytics

## Markets
MVP: 1X2, Over/Under, BTTS. Phase 2: Asian handicap, corners, cards, HT/FT, player props e live.

## Implied probability
Per quota decimale o: p = 1/o.

## Overround
Per outcome i: overround = somma di 1/o_i.

## Movement
Delta = current - opening.
DeltaPct = ((current - opening) / opening) × 100.

## Features
Opening, current, closing, min, max, volatility, velocity, reversal, consensus movement e bookmaker divergence.

## Best price
Per ogni match/market/selection identificare la quota corrente migliore con timestamp e provenance.

## Historical chart
Asse X timestamp, asse Y quota, una serie per bookmaker/selection.

## Limitation
Movement e implied probability sono misure descrittive e non garantiscono l'esito futuro.
