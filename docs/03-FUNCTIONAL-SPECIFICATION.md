# 03 — Functional Specification

## Modules
### Dashboard
KPI, partite del giorno, movimenti quote, data freshness e alert.

### Matches
Today, upcoming, live, results, ricerca, filtri e dettaglio.

### Odds
Explorer, movements, best price, history e confronto bookmaker.

### Competitions
Paesi, competizioni, stagioni, fasi e giornate.

### Teams
Profilo, forma, classifica, casa/trasferta, statistiche e storico.

### Standings
Snapshot storici per competizione, stagione, giornata e data.

### Analytics
Market, teams, form, models e backtesting.

### Data Operations
Providers, ingestion runs, errors, reconciliation e data quality.

### Admin
Users, plans, settings, permissions e audit.

## Filters
Date, country, competition, season, team, bookmaker, market, live/pre-match, odds range e movement threshold.

## Search
Global search + command palette Ctrl/Cmd+K.

## Export
CSV, JSON, Excel e PDF nei piani che lo consentono.
